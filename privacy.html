Privacy policy

DAILY · Last updated October 16, 2026

The short version

DAILY collects nothing. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads. Your habits and everything you log stay on your device.

Your habit data

Habits, timer sessions and marks are stored only on your device. DAILY does not send them anywhere.

Calendars (Plan my day)

Plan my day is off until you turn it on. When it is on:

  • DAILY reads the calendars on your device to find when you're busy. This happens on your device; nothing is sent to DAILY's maker or anyone else.
  • DAILY adds habit blocks only to a calendar of its own, named DAILY, and never changes or deletes anything in any other calendar.

Google Calendar

If you choose Connect to Google Calendar, you sign in with Google and DAILY asks for one permission: to create its own DAILY calendar in your Google account and manage the events in that calendar. Google does not let DAILY see or change your other Google calendars.

DAILY talks to Google directly from your device. Your Google sign-in is kept in your device's keychain. DAILY's maker never receives your Google data or sign-in.

DAILY's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To disconnect, tap Disconnect Google in DAILY, or remove DAILY at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The DAILY calendar stays in your Google account until you delete it.
