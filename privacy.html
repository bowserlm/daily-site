Privacy policy

DAILY · Last updated November 2, 2026

The short version

DAILY collects nothing. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads. Your habits and everything you log stay on your device.

Your habit data

Habits, timer sessions and marks are stored only on your device. DAILY does not send them anywhere.

Calendars (Plan my day)

Plan my day is off until you turn it on. When it is on:

  • DAILY reads your calendars to find when you're busy. This happens on your device; nothing is sent to DAILY's maker or anyone else.
  • DAILY adds habit blocks only to a calendar of its own, named DAILY, and never changes or deletes anything in any other calendar.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, you sign in with Google and DAILY asks for these permissions:

  • Make and manage its own DAILY calendar. This is the only calendar DAILY can change. Google enforces that.
  • See the list of your Google calendars, so you can choose which ones count as busy.
  • View the events on your calendars. Read-only. DAILY uses the start and end time of each event, whether it was cancelled, what kind of event it is, and whether you declined it, so habit blocks go around your real commitments. It does not request event titles, descriptions, locations or other guests through this read.

DAILY talks to Google directly from your device. Event times are used on your device while it plans and are not stored. Your Google sign-in is kept in your device's keychain. DAILY has no server, and its maker never receives your Google data or sign-in. DAILY does not share Google data with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

DAILY's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To disconnect, tap Disconnect Google in DAILY, or remove DAILY at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The DAILY calendar stays in your Google account until you delete it.
